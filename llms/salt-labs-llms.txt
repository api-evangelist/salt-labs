# Salt Labs

> Salt Labs is the New York company behind Salt, an employee rewards and loyalty benefit for hourly
> and frontline workers. An employee earns one Salt for every hour worked, tracked in the Salt
> Rewards mobile app, and redeems accumulated Salt for merchandise, travel, experiences, education
> benefits, and investments such as stock or treasury bonds. Salt is sold to employers in
> transportation, contact centers, hospitality and parking as a retention program modeled on airline
> miles. Founded in 2022 by Jason Lee and founding members of DailyPay; acquired by Chime Financial
> in June 2024 and now operating as "Salt, a Chime Company", with Jason Lee leading Chime Enterprise.

## What an agent should know first

- **Salt Labs publishes no public API.** There is no developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI,
  AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, MCP server or agent card. The full site sitemap (30 URLs, read 2026-08-26)
  contains no developer, docs, or API section, and no api./developer./docs. subdomain resolves.
- **Do not confuse this company with three others that share the name.** Salt Security
  (salt.security) is an API-security vendor with its own developer portal; SaltStack /
  Salt Project (saltstack) is infrastructure automation; github.com/salt-labs (saltlabs.tech)
  is an unrelated cloud-native devops group. None of them is saltlabs.com.
- **Its parent's API is not its API.** Chime Financial runs a partner API at
  developer.chime.com (documented for trusted partners, OAuth 2.0, user/account/transaction
  resources). That contract names Chime, not Salt, and is profiled separately under the Chime
  provider — it must not be attributed to Salt Labs.
- **The hosts answer honestly.** www.saltlabs.com (Webflow), app.saltlabs.com (the Salt App
  Backend) and status.saltlabs.com each return a discriminating 404 for a path that does not
  exist, so every absence recorded in this profile is a measured absence, not a blocked probe.
  app.saltlabs.com serves the two mobile deep-link association documents
  (/.well-known/apple-app-site-association, /.well-known/assetlinks.json) for the Salt Rewards
  iOS and Android apps, and nothing else at /.well-known/.
